LAB 3: COMPONENT MODELLING & ARCHITECTURAL PATTERN SELECTION

Scenario: Self-Service Coffee Kiosk System

Architecture Selection: Layered Architecture

Reason 1: Layered architecture separates the presentation layer, business logic, and data storage. The presentation layer handles touchscreen interactions, while the business layer manages orders and payments.

Reason 2: The architecture supports the kiosk's requirements, including menu and pricing storage, credit card payments, and receipt printing. Clear interfaces make the system easier to maintain.

Security Advantage: A dedicated Payment Service provides a separate boundary for payment processing. Sensitive card details should not be stored unnecessarily.

Performance Benefit: The components communicate through defined interfaces, making the ordering workflow straightforward and reducing unnecessary dependencies.
